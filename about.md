The **East Africa Community of Practice on Mountain Monitoring** is intended as a collaborative space for institutions and individuals involved in observing and understanding mountain environments in East Africa.

## Initial focus

The CoP starts with the **Mount Kenya area** and with monitoring related to **climate** and **atmospheric composition**. These are starting points rather than fixed boundaries. The community may expand to other mountain regions, environmental domains and forms of collaboration over time.

## What the CoP can support

- exchange of information on monitoring sites, measurements and methods;
- stronger links between participating organizations and experts;
- coordination and comparison of monitoring approaches;
- shared workshops, training, field activities and scientific meetings;
- development of joint projects and proposals;
- documentation and exchange through the website and GitHub wiki.

## Activities

The **GEOMountain Symposium** is the first dedicated activity represented on this website. Further meetings and collaborative activities can be added as separate sections without changing the overall site structure.
