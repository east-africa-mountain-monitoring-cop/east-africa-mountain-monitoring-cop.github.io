  mountain monitoring in East Africa.

The Community of Practice brings together people and organizations interested in sustained, high-quality observations in East African mountain environments.

The initial geographic focus is the **Mount Kenya area**, with an initial thematic focus on **climate** and **atmospheric composition**. The scope can grow as the community develops, including additional mountain regions, monitoring themes, shared projects, training activities and future meetings.

The aim is practical collaboration: connecting monitoring activities, exchanging methods and experience, improving access to information, and identifying opportunities for coordinated observations and joint initiatives.
